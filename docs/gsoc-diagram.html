GSoC 2026 — Persistent & Scheduled Firmware Upgrades
OpenWISP Firmware Upgrader · Status State Machines · Aditya Shandilya

New statuses introduced by this project: waiting NEW on UpgradeOperation — device was offline at upgrade time, persistent retry pending. scheduled NEW and cancelled NEW on BatchUpgradeOperation. All other statuses (queued, in-progress, success, failed, aborted) are existing.

Note: The waiting status badge name is pending UX review by the OpenWISP team (per GSoC Discussion: Persistent & Scheduled Upgrades Clarifications #1249).
